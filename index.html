How We Work

Agency. Advisory.

We work two ways: agency or advisory. You'll know which one before we start.

Agency

Your outsourced commercial arm

We represent your credits or your procurement mandate in market — origination, negotiation, and execution under a clear marketing agreement. Through discovery and negotiation, the market deals with Carbon Merchant; your name enters only at confirmation, with the counterparty you contract with.

Sometimes that means acting as your outsourced commercial arm; sometimes as the broker, the bridge between buyer and seller, facilitating deal flow and strengthening the market for everyone in it.
Advisory

Your commercial bench

Retained or project-based advisory on structure, pricing logic, and market timing — for teams that need a desk's judgment without building one.

Where a mandate needs more than a desk, we bring in trusted specialist partners across the project lifecycle: technical developers, consultants, and verifiers. You contract with each directly; we coordinate the work and keep the standard consistent.

Judgment from live flow, not reports.
